What we stock

Twelve product categories, each with its own aisle in the StockWise warehouse.

✏️ Office Stationery

Pens, pencils, markers, highlighters, erasers, and correction tape.

📄 Paper and Notebooks

Copy paper, notebooks, sticky notes, index cards, and cardstock.

📎 Desk Accessories

Staplers, clips, scissors, rulers, tape, and desk organizers.

💻 Computers and Peripherals

Laptops, monitors, keyboards, mice, headsets, and webcams.

🔌 Cables and Charging

USB-C, HDMI, and Ethernet cables, chargers, hubs, and surge protectors.

🖨️ Printing and Ink

Ink and toner cartridges, labels, photo paper, and laminating pouches.

🪑 Furniture and Ergonomics

Office chairs, desks, laptop stands, monitor risers, and task lamps.

🗂️ Storage and Organization

File folders, binders, trays, storage boxes, and label makers.

🧽 Cleaning and Facilities

Cleaners, wipes, paper towels, trash bags, and hand soap.

☕ Breakroom and Kitchen

Coffee, tea, condiments, cups, plates, napkins, and snacks.

📮 Shipping and Mailing

Packing tape, bubble wrap, shipping boxes, mailers, and scales.

🦺 Safety and Maintenance

First aid kits, safety glasses, work gloves, batteries, and tools.
