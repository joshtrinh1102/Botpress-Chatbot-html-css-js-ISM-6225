🤖 VoltBot – Product Assistant

Our Botpress AI assistant answers questions about VoltEdge Electronics products.

About the Dataset

VoltBot searches a Botpress Table called VoltEdge_Products containing 10 products sold at VoltEdge Electronics. Each row includes:

Product_ID, Product_Name, Category, Brand, Price, Stock_Status, and Description.

The table is connected to a Knowledge Base, so VoltBot can answer questions about prices, categories, availability, and product features.

How to Chat

  1. Click the chat bubble in the bottom-right corner of this page.
  2. Type a question about any VoltEdge product.
  3. Be specific: mention a product name, category, or price range.

Sample Questions

  • "How much does the VoltBook Pro 14 cost?"
  • "What smartphones do you sell?"
  • "Is the BoomBox 360 Speaker in stock?"
  • "Which headphones have noise cancelling?"
  • "What is the cheapest smart home product?"
  • "Recommend a laptop under $1,000."
