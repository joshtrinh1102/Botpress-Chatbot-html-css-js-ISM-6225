🤖 StockWise - Product Assistant

Our Botpress AI assistant answers questions about our products.

About the Dataset

StockWise uses a Botpress Knowledge Base to answer questions about VoltEdge Electronics inventory. Our website displays 120 products from the inventory dataset.

Product information includes ProductID, ProductName, Category, ProductType, UnitPriceUSD, QuantityInStock, StockStatus, and ProductLocation.

Website inventory and Botpress knowledge are managed separately. Changes to website inventory may not immediately appear in StockWise's responses.

How to Chat

  1. Click the chat bubble in the bottom-right corner.
  2. Type a question about any VoltEdge product.
  3. Ask about product names, prices, categories, or locations.

Sample Questions

  • "How much do the black ballpoint pens cost?"
  • "What office stationery products do you sell?"
  • "Are the blue ballpoint pens in stock?"
  • "Where can I find the mechanical pencils?"
  • "What is the cheapest product in inventory?"
  • "Which products need to be reordered?"
