About Our Team

The ISM 6225 group behind the VoltEdge Electronics sales portal.

Our Project

VoltEdge Electronics is a fictional consumer electronics retailer with four Florida stores and an online shop. This static prototype shows the business theme for our final project: a sales and inventory web application with analytics dashboards and an AI-powered product assistant. The final version will be a dynamic application backed by a real database.
