About Our Team

The ISM 6225 group behind StockWise, an AI-assisted office supply inventory portal.

Our Project

StockWise is an inventory web application for an office supply warehouse. It tracks a catalog of 120 products across 12 categories, from office stationery and printing supplies to furniture, breakroom, and safety items, sourced from 7 suppliers and stored in 12 aisles organized by bay and shelf.

Each product record includes its price, quantity in stock, reorder level, stock status (In Stock, Reorder Needed, or Out of Stock), exact warehouse location, supplier, and inventory value. The site pairs an analytics dashboard and an inventory management page with the StockWise chatbot, built in Botpress, which answers questions about pricing, availability, product locations, and restocking using this same product catalog.
